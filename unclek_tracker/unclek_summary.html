You should enter as close the the entry price as possible. Chasing is NOT recommended.  * The trade entry MUST happen before Noon EST. If the entry price has not printed before noon, the trade setup is dead. * The stop and profit targets are recommendations; you choose how you exit and at what price. You do you. * If the 1.5x or 2x profit targets print before we enter the trade at the "entry price", the trade becomes much less probable. I would either trade a much smaller position or avoid the trade since the probability of profit has diminished significantly. * Trade your risk. Shares are the easiest way to trade this system. 1DTE options bought ATM on SPY, QQQ, and IWM can work as well. SPX - 0DTE ATM options can work as they hold their time value for most of the day.  * I ALWAYS trade the same size position. The risk parameters have been setup, you already know your risk exposure, so trade it properly and size accordingly.  * This setup works well with the daily pattern system. If we have a low-high day and get a bullish trade for the Morning Setup, both seem more likely to play out.
